What does MADHU do?

A business creates an AI employee, gives it a specific job, knowledge, language and personality, and MADHU allows that AI employee to perform repetitive business tasks.

MAYA :Maya can:

Call new leads
Speak Telugu/English
Answer business questions
Understand customer requirements
Qualify leads
Schedule appointments
Follow up
Update CRM
Send WhatsApp messages
Transfer important calls to humans


V1 → Education

Target:

Coaching institutes
Training institutes
Colleges
EdTech companies
Skill-development centers

Later MADHU can expand to:

Real Estate → Insurance → Healthcare → Travel → Automotive → Financial Services

First AI Employee: MAYA

Example conversation

Customer:

"నేను Data Analytics course గురించి తెలుసుకోవాలి."

Maya:

"తప్పకుండా. మీరు beginner level నుంచి start చేయాలనుకుంటున్నారా, లేక already experience ఉందా?"

Customer:

"నేను beginner."

Maya understands:

Intent: Course enquiry
Interest: Data Analytics
Experience: Beginner
Lead priority: High
Next action: Schedule counselling
